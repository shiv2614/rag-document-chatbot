BohoCandle Co. Product and Policy FAQ

About BohoCandle Co.
BohoCandle Co. is a handcrafted soy wax candle and boho home decor brand. All candles are made using 100% natural soy wax, cotton wicks, and premium fragrance oils. The brand focuses on aesthetic, eco-friendly home decor products inspired by bohemian design.

Candle Burn Time
Our standard jar candles (200g) have an average burn time of 40 to 45 hours when burned correctly. Burn time can vary depending on room temperature, drafts, and how long each burning session lasts.

How to Burn a Candle Properly
Always trim the wick to about 5mm before each burn. Let the candle burn long enough on the first use for the wax to melt evenly across the entire top surface, usually 2 to 3 hours. Avoid burning a candle for more than 4 hours at a stretch. Keep candles away from drafts, curtains, and flammable objects.

Fragrance Options
BohoCandle Co. candles are available in several fragrance families including floral (rose, jasmine, lavender), woody (sandalwood, cedar), fresh (citrus, ocean breeze), and warm (vanilla, cinnamon spice).

Shipping Policy
Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days. Standard shipping takes 4 to 7 business days depending on location. Currently, BohoCandle Co. ships only within the country and does not offer international shipping.

Return and Refund Policy
Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. Customized or personalized items are not eligible for return.

Candle Safety Tips
Never leave a burning candle unattended. Keep candles out of reach of children and pets. Place candles on a heat-resistant, flat surface. Do not move a candle while the wax is liquid.

Packaging
BohoCandle Co. candles are shipped in eco-friendly packaging with minimal plastic use. Glass jars can be reused for storage or as small planters after the candle has finished burning.

Wholesale and Bulk Orders
BohoCandle Co. offers bulk pricing for wholesale buyers, gifting companies, and event organizers. Minimum order quantities and pricing tiers vary based on product type and order size. Interested buyers can reach out through the contact page for a custom quote.

Contact and Support
For questions about orders, shipping, or product care, customers can reach BohoCandle Co. through the contact form on the website or via the brand's Instagram page.
